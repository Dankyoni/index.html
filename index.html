About

I got into this work sideways.

I did SDGs field research for my master — working with immigrant students in Japanese schools on language, identity, and belonging. It taught me something I've carried into every commercial role since: the hardest problems aren't technical — it's about creating a space where people feel comfortable enough to show up as themselves — and then comes the translation: of culture, context, and trust.

I grew up in a mountain village in south China, the first in my family to go to university, work abroad and travel the world. Over almost six years in consulting, key account management and e-commerce growth across Japan and Asia-Pacific, I've learned that markets have personality, accounts have histories, and the best commercial decisions need someone who can hold both the data and the human context. That is what I do: I translate, across cultures and between a brand and the partners who grow it.

In 2025 I spent a year on the road across Europe, South America, the US and Asia, then moved to Germany and reached German B2 in eight months. Wherever I go, the pattern is the same: arrive somewhere new, learn from the people there, earn their trust, then deliver. Read my journey →
